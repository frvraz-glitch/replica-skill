# Protocol builder: from frame to SMART

Every Discipleship post ends with something he can do. Build it in this order.

## 1. Frame → behaviour
Write the frame in one line, then the single behaviour that embodies it. One behaviour per post. A series can stack them.

## 2. SMART, the FRVR way

| Letter | Question | FRVR rule |
|---|---|---|
| **S**pecific | What exactly, where, when? | One action, one cue, one place |
| **M**easurable | How will he know he did it? | A yes/no or a number he can log |
| **A**chievable | Can he do it on his worst day? | The **Floor** must take ≤ 2 minutes |
| **R**elevant | Does it answer the root this series uncovered? | Tie it to the lie and the truth by name |
| **T**ime-bound | For how long, and when does he review it? | 7, 30 or 49 days (Life Reset 7x7), with a weekly review |

## 3. The ladder
- **Floor:** the 2-minute version, done even on the worst day.
- **Standard:** the real target.
- **Stretch:** a good day.
The ladder is stewardship, never worth. Hitting the Stretch earns nothing before God.

## 4. If-then plans
Write at least one: "If <cue or obstacle>, then I will <floor behaviour>." Implementation intentions are among the better-supported behaviour-change tools. Verify the current evidence and effect size in `frvr-evidence-library` before stating any number.

## 5. The grace clause
Unbroken: **"Miss the target? Hit the floor. Never miss twice."** Every protocol includes how to return, because the restart loop is itself a root FRVR addresses.

## 6. Make it social
Add one line of accountability: a brother, a comment check-in, the weekly review. Formation is never solo (SOCIAL face).

## 7. Safety
Stay within guideline-level advice. Hormones, fasting, supplements, mental health and medical topics carry a signpost (GP, counsellor, Lifeline 13 11 14). Never recommend unsafe or unlawful items.

## Template

```
PROTOCOL · <name>                 Answers the lie: "<lie>"  with  "<truth>"
Behaviour:   <one action>  ·  Cue: <when/where>
Floor:       <≤2 min>   Standard: <target>   Stretch: <good day>
If-then:     If <obstacle>, then I will <floor>.
Measure:     <Y/N or number> logged <where>
Duration:    <7 | 30 | 49> days · review every <day>
Grace:       Miss the target? Hit the floor. Never miss twice.
With:        <brother / comment check-in>
Evidence:    <claim → frvr-evidence-library, grade>
```
