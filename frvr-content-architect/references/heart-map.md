# The heart map: what the FRVR man can't quite say

These are working hypotheses, not facts about any one person. Test and refine them with real signals: comments, DMs, story polls and question boxes, survey answers, search phrases, Aziel's coaching conversations and his own story. Log new phrases in the vault so the map is built from the follower's words over time. Confirm the audience band (age, stage, faith background) from memory before targeting a piece.

## 1. The six layers of one man

| Layer | What it holds | Where we find it |
|---|---|---|
| **Says** | The surface sentence: "I'm tired", "I can't focus", "I'm fine" | Comments, search terms, captions he saves |
| **Does** | The pattern: scroll, restart, isolate, overwork, numb | Screen time, his routines, what he confesses |
| **Feels** | Restless, ashamed, invisible, behind, lonely, flat | DMs, late-night posts, lament language |
| **Fears** | Being found out, being ordinary, being alone, failing his family, God's disappointment | What he jokes about; what he never posts |
| **Believes** | "I am what I achieve." "I'm on my own." "If they knew, they'd leave." "God tolerates me." | The lie behind the pattern (why-ladder) |
| **Longs for** | To be known and still loved; to be a son; to matter; to belong; to be steady; to be free | The good desire God placed there (Ecclesiastes 3:11) |

Augustine named the longing layer: "You have made us for yourself, and our heart is restless until it rests in you" (*Confessions* 1.1, public domain).

## 3. Five root families

Most roots fall into five families. Each one is a disordered version of a good longing, and each has a gospel answer. Use these to give seasons their spine.

| Root family | The counterfeit it trusts | The good longing | The gospel answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Worth** | performance, looks, status | to be valued | loved before you achieve (Ephesians 1:4–6) |
| **Control** | planning, money, self-reliance | to be safe | the Father's care (Matthew 6:25–34) |
| **Comfort** | screens, food, lust, numbing | rest and relief | Christ's rest (Matthew 11:28–30) |
| **Belonging** | approval, image, a tribe at any cost | to be known | adopted, and a brother among brothers (Romans 8:15; Hebrews 2:11) |
| **Significance** | being extraordinary, legacy | to matter | made for good works prepared in advance (Ephesians 2:10) |

All references here are to be fetched in the BSB and read in context before use.
