# The Narrow Path Review: for His glory

> "Not to us, O LORD, not to us, but to Your name be the glory." (Psalm 115:1)
> "For we do not proclaim ourselves, but Jesus Christ as Lord, and ourselves as your servants for Jesus' sake." (2 Corinthians 4:5)
> References here are paraphrased or close to the text. Fetch the BSB before setting any of them on a design.

**The FRVR charter** (Aziel, 6 Nov 2026, verbatim): "may everything FRVR be for the glory of god, with full integrity truth and love, in a heart and mind of service and giving value, embedded with wisdom compassion authenticity grounded humility responsibility zeal passion, from fragmentation to integration, reinforcing and reminding of sonship identity, savior focus over self focus, on the narrow path that leads to life, deeply embedded and cross-checked with whole bible in context and verified interpretation and original author intent of each verse and chapter and book, with highest quality research support on all health claims, concepts, and protocols"

This is the first gate every FRVR AZ and FRVR Well decision passes through: themes, topics, wording, scripts, carousels, captions, design, brand, priorities and the use of time. It sits above the Architect Gate, the Bible and Literature Checks and every studio's Quality Gate. A piece that is beautiful, viral and accurate but fails here doesn't ship.

## 1. Why: the humility of God

The Father glorifies the Son (John 8:54; 17:1). The Son does nothing by himself and seeks the Father's glory, not his own (John 5:19, 30; 7:18; 8:50). The Spirit does not speak on his own: he glorifies the Son and makes him known (John 16:13–14). The triune God is a communion of self-giving love, each person pointing to the other. The Son, though in the form of God, emptied himself and humbled himself to the cross (Philippians 2:5–11).

Pride turns the other way. The serpent's offer was "you will be like God" (Genesis 3:5). The first Adam grasped; the last Adam emptied himself (Romans 5:12–21; 1 Corinthians 15:45–49; Philippians 2:6). The "old man" keeps glorifying the self in subtle and open ways, and in the last days people will be "lovers of themselves… having a form of godliness but denying its power" (2 Timothy 3:1–5). The world's three pulls are the desires of the flesh, the desires of the eyes and the pride of life (1 John 2:16).

So FRVR exists to **know Him and make Him known** (Philippians 3:10; Matthew 28:19–20). That means pointing past itself, as the Spirit points to the Son: "He must increase; I must decrease" (John 3:30). It is done from death and life: "you died, and your life is now hidden with Christ in God" (Colossians 3:3). Count yourselves dead to sin and alive to God (Romans 6:11). Take up the cross daily (Luke 9:23). Do it by the Spirit, not by striving (Galatians 3:3; 5:16, 25; Zechariah 4:6), and do all of it for the glory of God (1 Corinthians 10:31; Colossians 3:17, 23).

There is a way that seems right to a man, but its end is the way of death (Proverbs 14:12; 16:25). The gate is narrow (Matthew 7:13–14). So every decision is funnelled through it.

## 2. The ten questions

Ask each one of every piece. Mark it ✅ aligned, ◐ refine or ✖ realign.

| # | Question | Scripture | Signs it's drifting |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Glory: who is the hero, and who gets the last word?** | Ps 115:1; John 3:30; 2 Cor 4:5 | Aziel, the brand, the method or the follower's achievement is the climax. Christ is a garnish. "My journey" with no turn to Him. The logo is bigger than the Word |
| 2 | **Source: Spirit or striving?** | Gal 3:3; Zech 4:6; John 15:5 | Grind, hustle, "lock in", "ruthless", willpower as saviour, optimisation as identity, guilt-driven protocols, "become someone" |
| 3 | **Word: does it stand under Scripture, in context, with the whole counsel?** | 2 Tim 3:16–17; Acts 20:27 | Proof-texting, verses as decoration, missing repentance, holiness or the cross, Christian-flavoured self-help, a gospel without sin or a call without obedience |
| 4 | **Humility: is the tone humble, grounded, steady and consistent?** | Phil 2:3–8; 1 Pet 5:5–6; Matt 11:29 | Hype, superlatives, shock, edgy cleverness, clickbait, "secret", "unlock", "elite", "1%", flexing, a mood that changes from post to post |
| 5 | **Love: does it serve the follower, or use him?** | 1 Cor 13:4–7; 1 Thess 2:3–8 | Fear or shame used to drive a click, false urgency, a call to action that serves the funnel more than the man, engagement bait |
| 6 | **Idols: is anything subtly put in God's place?** | 1 John 2:16; 1 John 5:21; Exod 20:3–5 | *Flesh:* the body, aesthetics, comfort, food or sex as the goal. *Eyes:* trends, metrics, gear, lifestyle. *Pride of life:* status, being admired, the brand, the platform, being right. Also: the protocol, the experience, the creator |
| 7 | **World: is a worldly frame being borrowed?** | Rom 12:2; Col 2:8; 1 John 2:15 | Trend language taken in uncritically (maxxing, main character, villain arc, aura, alpha), film or superhero mythology as the frame rather than an illustration, Eastern or New Age carry-overs (energy, manifesting, chakras, "the universe"), self-mysticism |
| 8 | **Truth: are health and science claims at the highest evidence base, graded and humble?** | Prov 18:17; 1 Thess 5:21 | Ungraded claims, overclaiming ("proven", "cure"), a single study as settled, influencer myths, science used to prove theology |
| 9 | **Focus: does it serve the one thing?** | Luke 10:42; Matt 6:33; Phil 3:13–14 | It doesn't move a man from fragmented to whole in Christ. It exists for reach, novelty or variety. It is one more series, format, tool or offer than the mission needs |
| 10 | **Fruit: would it still be right if no one saw it?** | Matt 6:1–6; Gal 1:10 | It only makes sense for the algorithm, an audience or a reputation. It is done to be seen by men |

## 3. Wording: narrow-path swaps

| Drifting | Narrow path |
|---|---|
| become your best self / level up / unlock | be formed into Christ's likeness / grow in grace |
| lock in, grind, ruthless | abide, keep in step with the Spirit, faithful |
| my method / my framework | a way of following Jesus I'm learning |
| crush it, dominate, elite, 1%er | faithful, steady, servant, ordinary obedience |
| self-love, self-worth, manifest | loved by the Father; worth received; ask, seek, knock |
| optimise | steward |
| discipline makes you who you are | discipline trains who you already are in Christ |
| follow me / join my brotherhood | follow Jesus; walk with brothers |
| you need this protocol | here's a small practice that has helped |
| this changed my life | God used this to change me |

These are defaults, not bans. A word stays if, in context, it clearly points to Him.

## 4. Brand, name and logo: held with open hands

- The name, logo and look serve the message as a signpost, never as the subject. On any slide, Scripture and Christ outrank the logo in size and order.
- Aziel's story is a **testimony**: what God did, not what Aziel did. Use the Testimony shape: before → what God showed → what God did → what he's still doing → "come and see" (John 4:29; Mark 5:19).
- The account split (FRVR AZ around 70% testimony) is held in prayer. If the face starts to become the message, rebalance toward the Word and toward other men's stories.
- Metrics are information for stewardship, never worth. Pray "Audience of One" before posting (Brand Bible §3).

## 5. Severity

- **✖ Realign:** it crosses the Theological Line, puts the self, the brand or a created thing at the centre, borrows a worldly frame as the foundation, or makes an unsupported health claim. Rewrite, or don't publish.
- **◐ Refine:** the substance is right, but wording, emphasis, order or tone drifts (Christ is late, a striving word, hype, logo emphasis).
- **✅ Keep:** it points to Him. Note what makes it work, so others can follow it.

## 6. How to use it

- **Every brief** (Architect Brief) records one line: *How does this point to Him?*
- **Every review** reports the ten questions as a short table, then the swaps that fix each ◐ or ✖.
- **Every season** gets a focus check: list what to stop, not only what to start (Hebrews 12:1, lay aside every weight).
- **Brand, theology of weight and strategy** go to Aziel as decision cards (vault decision tiers). The reviewer recommends; Aziel decides in prayer.
