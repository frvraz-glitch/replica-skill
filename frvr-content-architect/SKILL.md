# FRVR Content Architect

Every FRVR piece belongs to one larger story. That story is the gospel arc that runs under the whole account: **how you were made → what broke → who restores it → how you are formed**. The grid is one tapestry. Each season is a chapter, each series a passage and each post a line. Nothing is posted that doesn't know where it sits, what came before it and what comes next.

This is the **architect stage** of the FRVR content pipeline:

brain dump → **architect** → research → choose format → design / edit → schedule → analyse

It decides *what* to say, *why it goes here* and *how the argument runs*. The studios that come after it (`frvr-design-studio`, `frvr-maxxing-studio`, `frvr-reel-studio`, `frvr-talking-head-studio`, `frvr-ebook-studio`) decide how it looks. They take its brief as their input.

## 0. Before architecting

00. **The Narrow Path comes first:** `references/narrow-path.md` (in the vault: `🧠 Brain/3 Temple/Frameworks/The Narrow Path.md`, with the working [[Glory Gate]] at `🧠 Brain/1 Command Centre/Glory Gate.md`; the vault copies win if they differ). Everything is for the glory of God. We know Him and make Him known, pointing past ourselves as the Spirit points to the Son (John 16:14; John 3:30; 2 Corinthians 4:5). Every theme, word, design and priority passes its ten questions before anything else. Christ is the hero, the work is done by the Spirit and not by striving, the tone is humble and steady, and the brand is held with open hands.
0. **Read the founding thesis first:** `references/integration-thesis.md`. FRVR's central message is **fragmented → whole: not balance, integration**. Christ is the one in whom all things hold together (Colossians 1:17), and he integrates every part of a young man of God, inside (spirit, mind, emotions, body, social life) and outside (relationships, work, calling, assignments). Every season, series and post serves this thesis and names the integration pair it works on (the `frame` field in the Thread Map). The thesis file has the Split / Collapse / Integrated rule, the Cross of Integration lens, the pair atlas, the theological care notes and Season 0.
1. **Load the brand model.** Memory and `frvr-maxxing-studio/references/framework.md` hold the Temple: Christ at the centre, four faces (SPIRIT · Received, SOUL · Renewed, SOMA · Stewarded, SOCIAL · Belonging), twenty F-facets, five pillars, the named practices (Life Reset 7x7, Unbroken, The Cave, The Code, Alignment Audit, Temple 101). It also holds the account split: FRVR AZ is testimony (about 70%) and FRVR Well is protocols and frameworks (about 30%).
2. **Open the Thread Map** (section 5). If none exists yet, start one from `assets/thread-map-template.csv`. Read the last 12 rows before you plan anything new.
3. **Read the heart map** (`references/heart-map.md`) for the follower: what he says, does, feels, fears, believes and longs for.
4. **Sources come from the libraries, not memory.** Verses come from the BSB, fetched (Bible Check). Claims come from `frvr-evidence-library`. Voices come from `frvr-voices-library`. Book ideas come from `frvr-book-brain`.

## 1. The method: Root to Fruit

> "Every good tree bears good fruit, but a bad tree bears bad fruit." (Matthew 7:17, paraphrased here; fetch the BSB before quoting.)
> Jesus reads the fruit to find the root (Luke 6:43–45), and the righteous man is a tree planted by streams of water (Psalm 1; Jeremiah 17:7–8).

Every piece moves in two directions. Full method, the why-ladder and a worked example are in `references/root-to-fruit.md`.

**The Descent (define the problem: fruit → root)**

| Layer | Question | What it names | Lens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. **Fruit** (symptom) | What does he see and say? | "I can't stay consistent." "I'm always tired." | Behaviour |
| 2. **Pattern** | When and how does it happen? | The loop: cue → craving → act → relief → shame | Habit science, stress physiology |
| 3. **Pain** | What does he feel underneath it? | Numbness, restlessness, loneliness, shame | Emotion, nervous system |
| 4. **Lie** | What does he believe that keeps it going? | "I am what I achieve." "I'm on my own." "God is disappointed in me." | Beliefs and schemas; the renewed mind (Romans 12:2) |
| 5. **Root** | What is he trusting, loving or fearing most? | Disordered love, an identity built on sand, a thirst sent to broken cisterns (Jeremiah 2:13) | The heart (Proverbs 4:23; Mark 7:21; Luke 6:45) |
| 6. **Longing** | What does he really want? | To be known, to be a son, to matter, to belong | Ecclesiastes 3:11; Augustine's restless heart |

**The Ascent (present the answer: root → fruit)**

| Stage | What it gives | Temple face it usually lands on |
|---|---|---|
| 1. **Truth** | The gospel answer to the lie: who God is and who you are in Christ. It is received, not achieved. | SPIRIT · Received |
| 2. **Frame** | A new way of seeing it: a frame of mind, a reframe, the upside-down kingdom move | SOUL · Renewed |
| 3. **Framework** | A model he can hold: the Temple, a ladder, a cycle, a matrix | SOUL / any face |
| 4. **Formation** | The practice and the protocol: Floor / Standard / Stretch, if-then plans, a rhythm | SOMA / SOCIAL / any face |
| 5. **Fruit** | The fruit of the Spirit growing where the symptom showed (Galatians 5:22–23). The fruit is His work, not our score. A SMART measure is only a stewardship tool for noticing it, with a review date | The face where the symptom showed |

The Descent goes down to the heart. The Ascent comes back up to the hands. A piece that only describes the problem is a lament with no turn. A piece that only gives a protocol is maxxing with a verse on it. FRVR does both, in that order.

Because the follower may not know he has the problem, **the Descent must name his experience better than he could**. The test is that he thinks "how did you know?" This is the Nathan move (2 Samuel 12:1–7): a story he recognises before he realises it's about him. It is never manipulation. "We have renounced secret and shameful ways… by setting forth the truth plainly" (2 Corinthians 4:2, paraphrased; fetch the BSB). We name what is true, and we never invent a fear in order to sell a fix.

## 2. Scales: the same arc at every size

One arc repeats at every scale, like a fractal. The metanarrative of Scripture (Creation → Fall → Redemption → Restoration) maps onto Root to Fruit:

| Act | Root to Fruit | What it shows | Science voice |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Design** (Creation) | the standard | How you were made to work | Physiology, psychology, how humans thrive |
| **Distortion** (Fall) | Descent: Fruit → Root | What broke and why | Mechanisms of the problem |
| **Deliverance** (Redemption) | Ascent: Truth | Christ: the finished work, the new identity | — (theology is not tested by science) |
| **Discipleship** (Restoration) | Ascent: Frame → Fruit | Formation, protocols, community | Behaviour change, habit research |

| Scale | Size | How the four acts appear |
|---|---|---|
| **Slide** | one idea | each slide does one job from one act |
| **Carousel or reel** | 7–10 slides / 30–90 s | all four acts, Descent then Ascent (`references/narrative-structures.md`) |
| **Series** | 3–9 posts on one theme | each post leans on one act or layer, and the series completes the arc |
| **Season** | 6–12 weeks | one face or one big root, with three or four series climbing through it |
| **Year / grid** | 4 seasons | the whole Temple: a full lap of all four faces, Christ at the centre of every one |

The full planning system (seasons, series, the grid, launch, weekly rhythm, how posts link) is in `references/grid-architecture.md`.

## 3. Modes

Pick the mode from the request. Say which one in one line, then work.

**A. Shape one idea** ("I want to post about X")
1. Run the why-ladder on X (`references/root-to-fruit.md` §2) until it reaches a lie and a longing. Show the ladder.
2. Place it. Which season and series does it belong to? Which act and layer does it carry? What does it link back to and forward to? If it fits no series, either make it Part 1 of a new one or park it in the Idea Bank. Never post it as a stray.
3. Write the **Architect Brief** (section 4) and hand it to the right studio.

**B. Build a series** ("a series on sleep / porn / purpose")
1. Run the Descent on the theme. Each layer that is strong enough becomes a post.
2. Lay out 3–9 posts so the series does the full Root to Fruit. The default 6-post series is in `references/grid-architecture.md` §3.
3. Give it a series name, a mark and numbering ("02 / 06"), one signature look and one lead magnet at the end.
4. Write one Architect Brief per post and add the rows to the Thread Map.

**C. Plan a season or the grid** ("plan the next 12 weeks", "the first 12 posts")
1. Choose the season's face or root from the Temple lap and the gaps the Thread Map shows.
2. Lay out series across weeks and the weekly rhythm, and plan grid rows as triptychs (`references/grid-architecture.md` §4–6).
3. Produce a season map: weeks × posts, each with series, part, act, layer, face, account, format and CTA.
4. Run `scripts/thread_check.py` on the planned rows before showing it.

**D. Audit what's there** ("does my grid connect?", "what's missing?")
1. Read the profile (with `social-post-reader`) or the Thread Map.
2. Back-fill a row for every post. Run `scripts/thread_check.py`.
3. Report the orphans, where it stalls in the Descent (all problem, no turn), protocol-only posts (no root, no Christ), faces that are missing, and broken links. Then propose bridge posts that tie the existing pieces together.

**E. Go deeper** ("go deeper on this", "why do men really…")
Run the full Descent with all six layers. For each layer, give the biblical lens, the science lens (as named concepts, with claims sent to `frvr-evidence-library` to verify) and the follower's own words (from comments, DMs, surveys or search phrases, never invented quotes). End with the root in one sentence and the longing in one sentence.

## 4. The Architect Brief (hand-off to every studio)

One per post. It sits above the studio's own brief.

```
ARCHITECT BRIEF · <series name> <part>/<total> · <account>
Season / series: <season> › <series>          Thread ID: <id>
Act · layer:     <Design | Distortion | Deliverance | Discipleship> · <Fruit…Longing | Truth…Fruit>
Face · facet:    <SPIRIT|SOUL|SOMA|SOCIAL> · <facet>     Pillar: <pillar>
He says:         "<the surface sentence, in his words>"
Underneath:      pattern → pain → lie → root → longing (one line each)
The turn:        <truth answering the lie> · verse ref (fetch BSB)
Frame:           <the reframe in one line>
Framework:       <model name, or "none in this post">
Protocol:        <SMART: floor / standard / stretch, if-then, measure, review date>
Evidence:        <claims to verify in frvr-evidence-library>
Links:           ← <previous thread ID: what this builds on> · → <next: what it sets up>
Grid:            row <n>, position <L|C|R>, look <look>
Next step:       <one: Scripture to read, a church or brother to go to, a prayer, or (sometimes) a resource>
Mirror line:     "<the line that makes him think 'how did you know?'>"
Points to Him:   <how this piece glorifies God and makes Christ the hero; the last word is His>
```

## 5. The Thread Map

The Thread Map is the memory of the whole tapestry: one row per post, planned or published. Template: `assets/thread-map-template.csv`. File it in the vault (FRVR Brain › the content or Command Centre area; list the vault and read its `CLAUDE.md` for the current folder before writing, and propose before filing). In a cloud session, keep it in the working folder and say where it is.

Columns: `id, status, date, account, season, series, part, of, act, layer, face, facet, pillar, format, surface, lie, root, longing, truth, verse, frame, framework, protocol, evidence, prev, next, grid_row, grid_pos, cta, offer`.

Run the checker on it after every plan and before every season:

```bash
python3 scripts/thread_check.py path/to/thread-map.csv
```

It reports orphans (no series or no links), series that don't complete Root to Fruit, Discipleship posts with no SMART protocol, Distortion runs with no turn, faces and acts out of balance, links that point to missing rows, gaps in the grid rows, and verse or evidence fields that are empty. It needs only the Python 3 standard library.

## 6. The Architect Gate

The Glory test is not traded off against craft. A piece that fails it doesn't ship, however well it scores elsewhere. Score each item 1–5 and fix anything under 4 before the brief goes to a studio. Run this on top of the studio's Quality Gate and the Bible and Literature Checks (`frvr-design-studio/references/checks.md`), not instead of them.

| Test | 5 looks like |
|---|---|
| **Glory** | It passes the Narrow Path's ten questions. Christ is the hero and gets the last word. Nothing in it is done to be seen by men |
| **Mirror** | The opening names his experience better than he could. He thinks "how did you know?" |
| **Depth** | The why-ladder reached a lie and a root, not just a second symptom |
| **Turn** | Christ is the turn, not the add-on. Truth comes before technique, and identity is received, not earned |
| **Logic** | Each slide or post follows from the one before, with no leaps and no filler. You could say the argument in five sentences |
| **Evidence** | Every claim has been sent to the evidence library and graded. Theology isn't "proved" by science; the science shows the measurable side |
| **Tangible** | There is one thing he can start tonight, written as SMART with a floor |
| **Thread** | It links back and forward, it is in the Thread Map, and the checker passes |
| **Grid** | It sits in a planned row and look, and the tapestry still reads at a glance |
| **Care** | No shame, no hype, no invented fear. There is a signpost where health or mental health comes up (GP, counsellor, Lifeline 13 11 14) |

## 7. Voice and guardrails

- Australian English. Plain words, one idea per line. The studios' word limits apply.
- **Diagnose patterns, never people.** "Many men find…", not "you have…". This is not clinical language and not a diagnosis.
- **The follower's words are found, not invented.** Mirror lines come from real comments, DMs, surveys, search terms and Aziel's own story. A drafted line is marked as a draft for Aziel to test.
- **Aziel's testimony is his to tell.** Draft the structure; his words fill it.
- **Honest urgency only.** No manufactured fear, scarcity or shame to drive a click.
- **Science and Scripture each get their own job.** Scripture tells us what is true about God and man. Research describes how the body and mind tend to work. Where they meet, say so. Where research is weak, say so.
