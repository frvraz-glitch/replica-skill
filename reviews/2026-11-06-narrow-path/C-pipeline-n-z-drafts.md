Reviewer: Claude (Narrow Path standard). Read-only review, 6 Nov 2026. Nothing in /home/user/frvr was edited.

## 1. Overall verdict

1. The scripted work (Week 1–4 Scripts, Men of the Book, Returning, Two Ditches, the testimony) is mostly Christ-centred, careful with context, graded on evidence and safe on messaging. It's the strongest part of the vault.
2. Most of the drift is in **pipeline cards that lag the cleaned drafts**: old hook lists still say "V-taper secret", "Winter arc", "lock-in", "grind" and "without getting soft".
3. Three things recur. **"Lock In"** is used as a series and product name. Funnel CTAs (AUDIT, RESET, TEMPLE) are bolted onto endings that should rest on Christ. And **Aziel's own numbers** fill about a third of the FRVR AZ slots.
4. The testimony is a real testimony of what God did. It needs refining, not realigning: the brand ("That's why I'm building FRVR"), the Audit and "subscribe" get the last word after the prayer, and the source script still has "spiritual suicide".
5. The Calendar has one storyline (the 7x7 weeks), but it is over-produced: about 54 pieces in 27 days. It also schedules posts on three Sundays that the scripts mark as Sabbath.
