## 5. Patterns

1. **Self as subject in titles:** "my schedule / my vision / my plan / my Day 0 / honest numbers". The substance is often good, but the title makes Aziel the hero. Swap: "my X" becomes "what God showed me in X" (Testimony shape).
2. **Striving vocabulary in the programme spine:** lock-in, hard reset, enough is enough, burning the boats, shipping, never miss twice. Swaps: lock-in → abide / returning / faithful; reset → return; burn the boats → leave the nets; never miss twice → return, don't restart; shipping → offering.
3. **Christ arrives as the second beat:** the hook is about the self and the verse comes in at the midpoint (day-1-of-my-7x7, count-the-cost). Reorder so identity in Christ opens the piece, which the Line requires.
4. **Verses lifted from context:** Luke 14:28 (discipleship to productivity), Luke 16:10 (money to focus), Gal 5 (nine as "7 marks"), Daniel 1 (diet over God's favour), Lev 11 (oysters). The 4 Nov checks caught a lot. Add a check for "does the hook say what the verse says?"
5. **Worldly frames:** film IP, habit-guru maxims (Clear), conquest imagery, a "Tibetan" lineage, scarcity funnels ("founding spots", "comment X").
6. **Health:** mostly graded and humble. Remaining needs: never use a single study as the hook, date and contextualise trials (PREDIMED 2018), and grade every tool promised (ADHD).
7. **Brand over Word:** "Free FRVR…", "the FRVR Well method", "app to keep men accountable", and a gold halo around Aziel on the thumbnail. Signpost, not subject.
