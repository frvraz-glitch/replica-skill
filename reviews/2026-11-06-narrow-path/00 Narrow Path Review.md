## 1. What's already pointing to Him

The heart is sound. Most of what needs to change is in the layer around it.

- **The Theological Line**, *The Principle of Returning*, *Beholding and Becoming*, and *Sonship First*. These are the rock the rest should be rebuilt on.
- **Aziel's testimony** is a real testimony of what God did, not a success story. The Testimony note and the long-form script follow the right shape.
- **The Scripture pieces:** Elijah, Peter, *What I do when God feels far*, *One-way ticket*, false refuges, inflate or erase, manifesting versus being made new, loved not earned, conviction versus condemnation, and the Returning wallpapers and Two Ditches.
- **The safeguards:** the Bible Check, honest evidence grades in most scripts, safe-messaging signposts, Aziel's words kept verbatim, the Secret Place score left to Aziel, Audience of One, no boosting, and Sabbath rules in the scripts.
- **The archetypes** (especially King and Priest) are rooted in Christ, and the Ebook Studio's "faith is not a wellness intervention" sets the right tone.

## 2. Where it drifts: six patterns

**1. The hero drifts toward the man and the brand** (questions 1 and 10)
- The Brand Bible story (§1) makes Aziel the subject ("the man, in public, doing the work"). It uses Gotham and Batman as the frame and never names Christ. The Master version already bans Gotham and says it wins any conflict.
- The brand pyramid has a belief row "In Aziel", built with "proof on screen… the Word handwritten daily". There is also moat and "impossible to copy" language.
- The master photo is an AI rebuild with a softened expression, and one prompt stages him "reading a worn Bible". Both cut against Matthew 6:1 and FRVR's own value "Integrity: one man".
- The testimony script gives the brand the last word: after the prayer comes "That's why I'm building FRVR…", the Audit, "subscribe" and the logo.
- About a third of FRVR AZ slots are Aziel's own metrics. One thumbnail puts a gold halo around him. "My 49-day plan" uses Luke 14:28 as a productivity verse, when the passage is about the cost of following Jesus (14:33).

**2. Striving words survive after being rejected in principle** (question 2)
- Lock-in (as a series, product and Week 5 name), hard reset, ruthless reset, "come back dangerous", grind, winter arc, burn the boats, "without getting soft", "transmute".
- In the app design, the Spirit's fire and the Father's light grow with the user's consistency. That makes God's presence a reward for performance, and it contradicts the vault's own Gamification note. This is the most important theological fix in the review.

**3. Worldly frames used as foundations** (questions 7 and 6)
- Gotham and Batman ("what I do defines me" is works-identity in so many words), Lion King, Rocky IV, Return of the King, "Winter arc".
- "Maxxing" as the name of sacred practices: "Repentance Maxxing", "Die to Self Maxxing".
- A *Tibetan 5* follow-along carries chakra lore. That jars badly with a yogi-to-Christ testimony.
- "1%er" and "most disciplined men alive". The V-taper piece makes the look the goal and uses 1 Corinthians 6:19–20 to back it.

**4. The gates measure reach, not glory** (questions 5 and 10)
- The Test phase scores "Secret Sauce: 100x viral potential" first. There is no Glory score.
- The AI Team's Fit gate requires "a 100x angle". The growth agent "grows the reach" with a 0–100 grade that gives nothing for pointing to Christ. The brand guardian "guards the brand's glory". Captions start from "stop the scroll". Learn counts follows and DMs, with no fruit signals.
- Funnel CTAs (AUDIT, RESET, TEMPLE) are added after endings that close on Christ, including the porn and relapse pieces. The Alignment Audit is promoted six or more times in 12 days. "Founding spots" relies on scarcity.

**5. Order and truth slips** (questions 3 and 8)
- **Order:** *Operating Frameworks* sets "SOMA first… SPIRIT third", against Sonship First, Matthew 6:33 and the Lifetime Vision.
- **Scripture:**
  - "Biblical fuel: oysters" uses a food the Law called unclean (Lev 11:10–12). Under the new covenant it isn't sin to eat them (Mark 7:19), but "biblical" is the wrong label.
  - Habakkuk 2:2 is used for goal tracking.
  - Sabbath is reframed as recovery for gains.
  - A weekly fasting post sits against Matthew 6:16–18.
  - The Passion Translation is used for declarations.
- **Health claims:**
  - Ungraded claims sit in a note that feeds the app: "carnivore-primal", cold for dopamine, "Heal your nervous system".
  - "138 studies point the same way" overstates a moderate effect.
- **Facts:** "Seven years with young men" conflicts with other notes.

**6. Focus: the system itself is fragmented** (question 9). This is the same problem FRVR exists to heal.
- Two challenge frames run side by side (30-day lock-in and 7x7 / 49 days). There are three funnels (AUDIT, LOCKIN, TEMPLE) and three pillar counts (3, 4 and 5).
- There are many frameworks and taxonomies, 23 agents, about 9 separate quality rubrics and up to 9 platforms.
- The plan is about 11 posts a week, with two pieces a day for 27 days and none of the 30 days scheduled.
- The Week 1 decks exist in four render pipelines. The gym-refuge story is told four times, and there are a dozen duplicate pairs.
- The calendar schedules posts on three Sundays (15, 22 and 29 Nov), against the scripts' own Sabbath rule.

## 3. The one change that fixes the most: one Glory Gate, first in every gate

One shared file, read by every skill, agent and studio, which runs before any craft score:

> **The Glory Gate (runs first; it is not traded off against craft).** Mark each ✅/◐/✖. Any ✖ stops the piece; any ◐ is fixed with the Narrow Path swaps before the craft scores are read.
> 1. **Hero:** Christ is the hero and has the last word. Aziel, the brand, the method or the follower's achievement is never the climax.
> 2. **Source:** it calls a man to abide and keep in step with the Spirit, not to grind or earn. Identity is received before any practice is given.
> 3. **Word:** Scripture in context, within the whole counsel; never decoration.
> 4. **Humility:** humble, steady and plain. No hype, "secret", "unlock", "elite" or "1%".
> 5. **Love:** it serves the man and doesn't use him. No fear, shame, false urgency or bait.
> 6. **Idols and the world:** nothing in God's place, and no worldly frame as the foundation.
> 7. **Audience of One:** would it still be right if no one saw it?
>
> Record "Points to Him: …". Claude marks items 1–6; item 7 and the Secret Place belong to Aziel.

Where it goes: `frvr-test` (a Glory score first; under 7 means fix), the AI Team Operating Manual §5, the caption checklist, the growth agent (gated on "Points to Him"), the brand guardian ("God's glory", Narrow Path first), every studio's quality gate and `checks.md`, and the Content Brief (question 0). The Content Architect already runs it.

**Merge**
- Conviction vs condemnation ×2; Elijah ×2 (and fix the Part 2 numbering clash with Peter); neglect compounds ×2; watching the wind ×2 plus old man / new man (retire the absorbed cards)
- Rest vs escape + you don't need rest; struggle isn't your identity + saved and still struggle (posted six days apart)
- Comparison + a week of no comparison; never miss twice + a smaller day; 30 days done + day 1 vs day 29; fear of failure + I'm launching (around Zech 4:10)
- App prototype + building an app; founding spots + join round 2 (one invitation, with no scarcity)
- Same-day pairs, pick one each: lose my dad or a presence; guru shrine or manifesting
- The gym-refuge story told four times: keep two. Temple 101: keep two of four. Alignment Audit promos: keep two of six or more
- The Week 1 decks: keep one render pipeline and archive the other three

**Calendar**
- No posts on Sabbath.
- Fill the open slots from the ready Word pieces (new every morning, no condemnation, the rock and the clay) rather than more of Aziel's metrics.
- Reduce to three grid posts a week per account.
