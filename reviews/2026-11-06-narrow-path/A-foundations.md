# Narrow Path Review, Slice A: Foundations (2 Identity · 4 Brand · 3 Temple)

Reviewer: Claude (read-only; nothing in the vault was edited). Standard: `frvr-content-architect/references/narrow-path.md` and `3 Temple/Frameworks/The FRVR Theological Line.md`. 78 documents reviewed.

## 1. Overall verdict

1. The theology underneath FRVR is sound and often beautiful. The Theological Line, Returning, Beholding and Becoming, the Archetypes, Headship and Testimony point clearly to Christ, and they guard well against legalism, lawlessness and the New Age.
2. The drift is in the **brand layer**, not the doctrine. Older or marketing-sourced notes still make Aziel and the brand the hero. Examples: the Gotham story in the Brand Bible, "Belief in Aziel", "proof on screen", an AI-rebuilt face, and the monogram and press kit before there is any ministry fruit.
3. Two foundations contradict each other on the most important question, what comes first. Sonship First and the Lifetime Vision say SPIRIT and sonship first. Operating Frameworks and the FACES overviews say "SOMA first… SPIRIT third".
4. Worldly frames survive in the vocabulary even after they were rejected in principle. Examples: maxxing, lock-in, "ruthless", "come back dangerous", S-tier, "secret sauce", Gotham and Batman, "transmute" and "life force", and "empath".
5. The system is scattered. Four faces, five pillars, five archetypes, three Trinity images, four maturity stages, the 7x7, Temple 101, AZ Align, the Alignment Audit, 67 templates, six sub-brands and two calendars all exist before the first client. Lay aside weight before adding (Hebrews 12:1).

## 4. Decision cards for Aziel

**Card 1: What is FRVR AZ, a personal brand or a witness?**
FRVR AZ is described everywhere as a "personal brand", "founder brand" or "founder who vouches", with a monogram, nameplate, press kit and "Founder" as the bio's identity line. The tagline "FRVR: forever well, forever free" gives the brand what belongs to Christ (John 8:36).
*Recommendation:* keep the account, and rename its role in every document to "Aziel's testimony: what God has done and is doing" (Mark 5:19). Make the AZ bio identity "follower of Jesus", not "founder". Shelve the personal insignia. Change the tagline to "Free indeed, in Christ" or drop "forever free". Keep the FRVR name with open hands.

**Card 2: Retire Gotham and Batman completely?**
The Brand Bible keeps them as the "underlying concept". The Master bans "DC Gotham language" and says it wins any conflict. A photo is still titled "Gotham".
*Recommendation:* retire them fully. The Firmament (Psalm 19) and the Lion and the Lamb already carry the night-to-dawn story biblically. Rewrite Brand Bible §1 in the Testimony shape, with Christ as the turn and the hero.

**Card 3: The face. AI-rebuilt master photo and AI-staged scenes?**
The master cut-out is a "GPT Image 2.5 forest rebuild" with composited jewellery and a softened expression, and the prompts stage "reading a worn Bible".
*Recommendation:* replace the master with a real photograph, imperfect is fine. Never use AI images of Aziel in devotional acts (Matt 6:1). AI may make settings and objects, labelled as AI. This matches the value "Integrity: one man… no staged perfection".

**Card 4: Drop "maxxing" as a product line and series?**
"Temple-maxxing", "Maxxing guides" and the maxxing templates sit in Sonship First's product list.
*Recommendation:* drop it. It's trend vocabulary rooted in looksmaxxing, which Aziel himself names as "a vain movement" in his testimony. Use "Stewardship guides" or "Blueprints".

**Card 5: What comes first, SOMA or sonship?**
Operating Frameworks and the FACES overviews say SOMA → SOUL → SPIRIT. Sonship First, the Lifetime Vision (SPIRIT first, Matt 6:33) and the Theological Line say identity first.
*Recommendation:* the foundation is always Christ and sonship (Rock → Behold → Return → Become). SOMA can be the gentlest first practice for a dysregulated man ("a practical on-ramp"), never the first priority. Edit the three notes to agree.

**Card 6: The size of the vision**
The vision includes 1M followers, $100k a month, five sub-brands, an NDIS practice, an app, seven children and residences across continents.
*Recommendation:* keep it in a "Vision held in prayer" note (Hab 2:2; Prov 16:9). Take the reach and revenue stretch targets out of the Brand Bible and the operating documents, and replace them with faithfulness measures: posts made in obedience, men served, men in a church.

**Card 7: Church covering before launch**
"Church covering and mentor" is still open. The pilot starts 12 Jan 2027, and the ministry includes rites, inner-healing prayer and deliverance safeguards.
*Recommendation:* name a local church and a pastor or elder before the pilot, and put FRVR's teaching and rites under that oversight (Heb 13:17; Acts 13:1–3).

**Card 8: One spine, not many**
FRVR has four faces with twenty facets, five pillars, five archetypes, a three-image Trinity, Son → Bridegroom → Father, Rock → Behold → Return → Become, the 7x7, Temple 101, AZ Align, the Alignment Audit™, 67 templates, eleven series tags and two calendars.
*Recommendation:* for this season, the public spine is Christ at the centre → the four faces → the 7x7. Retire the five-pillar taxonomy. Keep the archetypes and the stages as depth, not entry points. Freeze new frameworks, templates and series until the floor cadence holds for 8 weeks (Luke 10:42).

**Card 9: Which translation for declarations and handwriting?**
Goals use NKJV plus The Passion Translation, and "biblical affirmations (TPT style)". TPT is widely criticised as a paraphrase with added content.
