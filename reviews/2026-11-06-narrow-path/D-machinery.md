# D. The machinery: skills, agents, studios and content-system docs

Reviewer's slice: the instructions that generate FRVR content. Read-only review against `frvr-content-architect/references/narrow-path.md` (ten questions) and `3 Temple/Frameworks/The FRVR Theological Line.md`. Offered humbly: the reviewer recommends; Aziel decides in prayer.

## 1. Verdict (5 lines)

1. The theology inside the machine is strong. Bible Check, the Theological Line, the Maxxing premise ("a son starts from Someone"), the Ebook rule that "faith is not a wellness intervention", and the Secret Place score reserved for Aziel are all excellent.
2. But the **operating goal written into the gates is reach, not glory**. The scores Claude actually computes are Secret Sauce ("100x viral potential"), hook, retention, save/share, flair and the follower target. No gate outside the new Architect skill asks "who is the hero, and who gets the last word?"
3. The machine pulls its craft from outlier creators ("100x", "outlier multiple", "the best talking heads in his niche"), and some of their worldly frames are kept as foundations: "maxxing" as the series name over sacred practices, the archetypes, "1%er", trend-jacking, "identity threat" as a comment trigger.
4. The persona layer (23 angel-style "-el" agents, "CEO", a crown, "the brand's glory") raises fair discernment questions. These are questions for Aziel, not verdicts.
5. The system is **far bigger than the mission needs** at zero followers: 23 agents, 23 repo skills, 12 studio skills, 9 platforms, 11 posts a week, 25 formats, 61 Maxxing titles and about 9 different quality rubrics. Every one of those adds a place where drift can creep in. One gate with Glory first, two platforms and a smaller core team would serve "the one thing" more faithfully.

**Counts (48 files or items reviewed):** ✖ Realign 5 · ◐ Refine 30 (one of them, the agent names, is a question) · ✅ Keep 13.

## 3. Gates: what to add to every quality gate

Today about nine different rubrics exist: Operating Manual §5, frvr-test (five scores plus the depth check), the caption checklist (eight points plus pithiness), Yosiel's 0–100 grade, Hodiel §14, and the design, reel, talking-head, wallpaper, ebook and Architect gates. Only the Architect gate checks Glory. The proposal is **one shared block at the top of every gate**, kept in one file (`narrow-path.md` §7 "The Glory Gate") and referenced, not copied, by every skill.

**Proposed wording (paste as the first rows of every gate):**

> **The Glory Gate (runs first; it is not traded off against craft).**
> Mark each ✅ / ◐ / ✖. Any ✖ stops the piece, however well it scores elsewhere. Any ◐ is fixed with the Narrow Path swaps before the craft scores are read.
> 1. **Hero:** Christ is the hero and has the last word. Aziel, the brand, the method or the follower's achievement is never the climax.
> 2. **Source:** it calls a man to abide and keep in step with the Spirit, not to grind or earn (Galatians 3:3; John 15:5). Identity is received before any practice is given.
> 3. **Word:** Scripture is read in context, within the whole counsel (repentance, the cross, holiness), and never used as decoration.
> 4. **Humility:** humble, steady and plain. No hype, superlatives, "secret", "unlock", "elite" or "1%".
> 5. **Love:** it serves the man and doesn't use him. No fear, shame, false urgency or engagement bait. The CTA serves him first.
> 6. **Idols and the world:** nothing in God's place (body, metrics, brand, creator, protocol), and no worldly frame (maxxing, main character, alpha, aura, manifesting) used as the foundation.
> 7. **Audience of One:** would it still be right if no one saw it? (Matthew 6:1)
>
> Record one line on the piece: **Points to Him:** "<how>". Claude marks items 1–6. Item 7, and the Secret Place, belong to Aziel.

**Where it plugs in:** `frvr-test` (as the `Glory` score, first), Operating Manual §5 (as the first gate), `frvr-caption` (checklist items 9–10), `yosiel-growth` (Points to Him 20, a gate on its own), `hodiel-brand-guardian` (step 0), `checks.md` §4 and every studio's gate (a first row), `CS/04 Shape/Content Brief.md` (question 0), and `quality_gate.py` (machine-check the Narrow Path §3 drift words as warnings: level up, unlock, lock in, grind, ruthless, elite, 1%, crush, dominate, optimise, manifest, self-love, "my method").

## 4. Focus: consolidate, retire or pause

Hebrews 12:1 calls us to lay aside every weight. FRVR starts both accounts from zero, and the machine is sized for a media company.

1. **One pipeline, one name.** Two descriptions of the flow coexist. The repo runs Ideate → Test → Align → Schedule → Learn; the claude.ai studios run "brain dump → research → … → schedule (Blotato) → analyse"; and the Architect adds a stage. Make the Architect the *Shape* step inside Ideate (it already writes the brief), use the repo's five phases everywhere, and retire every "Blotato" hand-off (in the design, reel and talking-head studios, and in frvr-publish's description).
2. **One gate.** Retire the separate 1–5 rubrics in favour of one shared FRVR Gate: Glory, then Word and Evidence, then Care, then Craft. Each studio keeps only its craft rows. This removes about eight places where drift can return.
3. **Pause seven of nine platforms for Season 0.** Instagram is home, plus one more (YouTube Shorts or TikTok). LinkedIn, X, Threads, Pinterest and Facebook stay on `pack` or pause. This also shrinks the caption desk's seven native versions per piece.
4. **Halve the cadence.** Move from 11 posts a week across two lanes to the Architect's triptych: three grid posts a week per account (Mirror · Turn · Tool), plus stories. Steady and consistent beats volume (Narrow Path Q4 and Q9).
5. **A smaller core team.** Keep about 8 agents active: Ezriel (dispatch), Omaniel (content), Amariel (words), Tzuriel and Marael (make), Sefiel and Emetiel (truth), and Hodiel merged into the Glory gate with Sefiel. Pause until there is real need: Shaariel, Chizkiel and Igriel (no clients or subscribers yet), Pathiel and Shefiel (not content), Achiel (until there are comments to tend), Miniel and Yosiel (merge into one "stewardship" review once there is data), and Yesodiel, Zerubiel and Sofriel (as on-call tools, not standing personas).
6. **Pause the libraries.** Freeze the Maxxing title map at the titles Season 0 uses (61 is more than a year's need). Use the 16 carousel concepts, 14 reel formats, 10 talking-head recipes and 13 wallpaper concepts as a menu, not a mandate. Stop adding creator studies ranked by outlier multiple until Season 0 has run.
7. **Pause the virality layer.** Pause `virality_predictor`, the Trial Reel hook tests and the 0–100 virality grade until FRVR has its own baseline, and then only on Aziel's yes. They train the machine to optimise the hook, which pulls against Q4 and Q10.
8. **Reframe the 10,000-follower goal** as information, and put faithfulness goals beside it: Sabbath kept, every piece prayed over, men pointed to church and Scripture, and Aziel's capacity protected.
